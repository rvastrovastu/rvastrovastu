Privacy Policy

RV Astro Vastu · Last updated November 19, 2026

RV Astro Vastu is a Vedic astrology, Panchang and Vastu app. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and how you can delete it. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell your information.

Astrology calculations run on your device. Every install starts with an anonymous guest account, identified by a random ID, so your profiles can be saved without an email.

Services that process your information

  • Supabase hosts our sign-in and cloud database (birth profiles and email address).
  • OpenAI, Groq, Google Gemini and OpenRouter (tried in turn, if one is unavailable) write answers when you use Ask AI. They receive your question, a summary of your birth chart and recent messages in that chat, and only once you agree in the app. They use it only to write the reply.
  • Stripe takes payment for astrologer consultations. Stripe receives your payment details. We do not store your card number.
  • Your astrologer, only when you book a consultation and agree to share your birth details, receives those details to prepare your reading.
  • RevenueCat checks your purchases with Apple or Google. It receives your random account ID and your purchase history, not your birth details.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play take Premium subscription payments. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Your phone's map service (Apple or Google) turns a city name you search for into coordinates.
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics) receives crash reports and app usage events, as described above.
  • Google Fonts may download font files, which shares your device's IP address with Google.
  • Our email delivery provider sends sign-in codes and booking messages to your email address.

These providers process information only to run their part of the app. They may store it in countries other than yours, including the United States.

Sharing

We share information only with the services above, with the astrologer for a consultation you book, when the law requires it, or with your instruction (for example when you share a report or your family's day on WhatsApp). What you share leaves the app under your control.

If you add family members' birth details, please make sure they are happy for you to do so.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and cloud profiles until you delete them. The record of each Ask AI question (its date and length) is deleted after 13 months. Cancelled bookings lose their name, phone, email, question and birth details after 2 years. Deleting a profile in the app removes it from the device and the cloud.

Deleting your account removes your profiles, Ask AI records and sign-in details within a few minutes. Consultation bookings are kept only as payment records (date, length, price and payment status), with your name, phone, email, question and birth details removed, because tax and accounting law requires it. Backups held by our hosting provider expire within 30 days. Crash reports and usage events are kept by Firebase for up to 90 days and up to 14 months respectively.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Account → Delete account. See Delete your account for other ways.
  • Access or correct your birth details at any time in the app, or email us for a copy of what we hold.
  • Location can be turned off in your phone's settings; you can still enter places by hand.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, India or California), you may have further rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to a data protection authority. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect their information. Consultations and purchases are for users aged 18 or over. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections, and our database only lets each account read its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your email account safe, since it is how you sign in.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
