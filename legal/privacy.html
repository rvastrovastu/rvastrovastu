Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PlanbhaAI ("PlanBhai," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name and email address, when you create an account
  • Password (stored securely as a hashed value — we never store or have access to your plain-text password)

Household & User Content

  • Household names, invite codes, and membership lists
  • Wall posts, comments, chores, calendar events, grocery lists, budget/expense entries, and party-planning details you or other household members create
  • Photos you choose to upload (e.g., to household wall posts or family memories), stored via our image hosting provider (Cloudinary)

Location

If you choose to use location sharing, we temporarily store your location so it can be shared with your household members. Location sharing is opt-in and can be turned off at any time in the app.

Contacts

If you grant permission, we access your device contacts solely to let you select people to invite to events or households. We do not upload, store, or transmit your contacts to our servers.

Calendar

If you grant permission, the App can add events you create to your device's calendar. This happens locally on your device.

Microphone & Speech Recognition

If you use voice input features, audio is processed using Apple's on-device or cloud speech recognition to convert your speech to text (e.g., to add a habit, chore, or event by voice). We do not separately store audio recordings.

Purchase Information

If you subscribe to PlanBhai Premium, purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive only confirmation of your subscription status to unlock premium features.

Usage & Diagnostic Data

We may collect basic technical information (such as app version and device type) to help us fix bugs and improve the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the core features of the App (households, chores, meal planning, party planning, budgeting, etc.)
  • To sync your household's shared content across devices and household members
  • To send you notifications you've opted into (e.g., chore reminders, event reminders)
  • To process and manage your Premium subscription
  • To maintain the security of your account and detect abuse

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With your household members: content you post to a shared household (chores, wall posts, calendar events, budgets, etc.) is visible to other members of that household, consistent with the app's design.
  • Service providers: we use third-party services to operate the App, including MongoDB Atlas (database hosting), Render (backend hosting), Cloudinary (photo storage), and Apple (in-app purchases, push notifications). These providers process data only on our behalf and under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of PlanBhai, our users, or the public.

4. Your Choices & Rights

  • You can edit or delete content you've created within the App at any time.
  • You can revoke permissions (location, contacts, calendar, microphone, photos) at any time via your device's Settings.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.
  • You can leave a household at any time, which removes your membership but does not delete content already shared with that household.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and content for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal or legitimate business purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

PlanBhai is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted network connections and hashed passwords — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through an in-app notice.
