Privacy Policy

RV Astro Vastu · Last updated October 20, 2026

RV Astro Vastu is a Vedic astrology, Panchang and Vastu app. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and how you can delete it. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell your information.

Information the app uses

InformationWhyWhere it is kept
Birth details you enter: name, gender, date, time and place of birth (with its coordinates and time zone)To calculate charts, dashas, doshas, compatibility, numerology and reportsOn your device, and in our cloud database linked to your account so they are not lost
Email address (only if you sign in)To send your sign-in code and to keep your account and Premium on a new phoneOur cloud database
Your current location (only if you allow it)To work out sunrise, Panchang timings and the Vastu compass for where you areOn your device only. It is never sent to us

Reminder settingsTo show festival and timing remindersOn your device only

Astrology calculations run on your device. Every install starts with an anonymous guest account, identified by a random ID, so your profiles can be saved without an email.

Services that process your information

  • Supabase hosts our sign-in and cloud database (birth profiles and email address).
  • RevenueCat checks your purchases with Apple or Google. It receives your random account ID and your purchase history, not your birth details.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play take payments. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Your phone's map service (Apple or Google) turns a city name you search for into coordinates.
  • Google Fonts may download font files, which shares your device's IP address with Google.
  • Our email delivery provider sends sign-in codes to your email address.

These providers process information only to run their part of the app. They may store it in countries other than yours, including the United States.

Sharing

We share information only with the services above, when the law requires it, or with your instruction (for example when you share a report). Reports you share leave the app under your control.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and cloud profiles until you delete them. Deleting a profile in the app removes it from the device and the cloud. Deleting your account removes everything linked to it within a few minutes; backups held by our hosting provider expire within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Account → Delete account. See Delete your account for other ways.
  • Access or correct your birth details at any time in the app, or email us for a copy of what we hold.
  • Location can be turned off in your phone's settings; you can still enter places by hand.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, India or California), you may have further rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to a data protection authority. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections, and our database only lets each account read its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your email account safe, since it is how you sign in.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
