Delete your account

RV Astro Vastu · Last updated October 20, 2026

You can delete your RV Astro Vastu account and all its data at any time. This removes your email address, every saved birth profile and every chart saved with your account. It cannot be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open RV Astro Vastu and tap the account icon at the top of the Home screen.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete permanently to confirm.

Your data is removed straight away and the app starts fresh.

Without the app

Email you@yourdomain.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the account and confirm within 7 days.

Have Premium? Your subscription is billed by the App Store or Google Play, and deleting your account does not stop it. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account, email address, birth profiles and saved charts.
  • Kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat: purchase records they must keep for tax and accounting. These are linked to a random ID, not your name or birth details.
  • Our hosting provider's backups expire within 30 days.
