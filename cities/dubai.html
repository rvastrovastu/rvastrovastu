Hindu calendar & Panchang in Dubai

If you live in Dubai, your Ekadashi, Purnima or Diwali can fall on a different day from the calendar your family uses in India. Nothing is wrong: a tithi starts at the same moment everywhere, but that moment lands on a different clock time, and sometimes a different date, where you are. India is 1 h 30 min ahead of Dubai all year.
