Hindu calendar & Panchang in San Francisco Bay Area

If you live in the San Francisco Bay Area, your Ekadashi, Purnima or Diwali can fall on a different day from the calendar your family uses in India. Nothing is wrong: a tithi starts at the same moment everywhere, but that moment lands on a different clock time, and sometimes a different date, where you are. India is 13 h 30 min ahead of the San Francisco Bay Area in January and 12 h 30 min ahead in July, because of daylight saving time.

For example, Kartika Amavasya begins on Monday, December 7 in the San Francisco Bay Area but on Tuesday, December 8 in India. Rows where the starting day is different are highlighted.
